## 📋 Production Summary

- **Target Style**: High contrast ink wash, graphic novel aesthetic, solid
  shadows.
- **Color Strategy**: Grayscale base with specific **Red** objects for focal
  points.
- **Media Mix**: Image Only (Scenes #1-#7)
