**Caption:** "민준의 뒷모습 - 좁은 골목길을 걷는 모습"

**Final Prompt:**
```
Cinematic slow-motion tracking shot following the back view of a 29-year-old Korean male office worker in white dress shirt and black pants, carrying laptop bag, walking through narrow alley in old urban neighborhood, peeling walls and aging houses on both sides, warm golden hour lighting creating long shadows, melancholic atmosphere, steady cam movement, shallow depth of field with bokeh background, realistic motion, 24fps cinematic feel, 4K quality --ar 16:9 --motion 3
```
