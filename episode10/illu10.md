## 🎥 Scene 6: 변화 - 붉은 간판 (Video)

**Context:** "구산구 거리의 변화 타임랩스." **Media Type:** Video (Runway Gen-3)
**Visual Strategy:**

- **Noir Conversion**: 거리는 여전히 낡았지만 활기참. 흑백 배경 위로 시간이 빠르
  게 흐름.
- **Motion**: 타임랩스 (사람들의 빠른 이동, 조명 변화).
- **Red Accent**: 하나둘씩 켜지는 가게들의 **'붉은색 "영업중" 네온사인(Red Open
  Signs)'**.

**[Final Video Prompt]** Cinematic noir animation style, Time-lapse of an old
street in Gusan-gu. Buildings are gritty black and white. People move fast as
blurry shadows. As time passes, multiple shops turn on their lights.
Specifically, bright Neon Red "OPEN" signs flicker on one by one, symbolizing
economic revival. The street transforms from dead to alive. 15 seconds.
